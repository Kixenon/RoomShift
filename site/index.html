The problem

A layout can look right and still work badly.

The desk ends up in the one dim corner. The fan blows on the wall. The router hides behind the fridge. The heater sits a hand’s width from the sofa. You only find out after you’ve carried everything across the room. RoomShift lets you find out first.

Five lenses

Look through the room, not at it.

1 · Air

Watch the breeze travel.

Particles ride the solved airflow out of the fan or AC, curl around the furniture and fade where the jet dies. Hover anywhere for the speed at head height, and see how many air changes an hour your windows really give you.

3D incompressible Navier–Stokes · 5 cm grid on WebGPU · BS 5925 ventilation

2 · Heat

Know where the warmth goes.

Heaters inject their real watts; warm air rises and leaves through open windows. A steady-state heat balance adds sun through the glass, people and appliances, then tells you what the room settles at and what it costs to run.

Boussinesq buoyancy · U-values · solar gain · running cost per month

3 · Light & sun

Play the day across your floor.

Drag the clock and the sun moves for your city, your date and the way your windows face. Light pools in lux, bounces off your finishes, and the desk gets checked against the 300–500 lux you need to work.

NOAA solar position · inverse-square lamps · lumen-method bounce

4 · WiFi

See the signal, wave by wave.

Wavefronts spread from the router and dim as they pass through things. A fridge costs 22 dB, a wardrobe 3. Drag the router and the whole field follows, live.

ITU-R P.1238 indoor path loss · per-material penetration loss · 2.4 / 5 GHz

5 · Sound

Hear the room before you’re in it.

A speaker’s level at every seat, plus how long the room rings. Swap the tile floor for carpet or add a sofa and watch the echo time fall.

Eyring reverberation · direct + diffuse field · barrier loss

Livability

Does it still work to live in?

Every change is scored against how people actually use rooms, then one click searches for a better arrangement that keeps the pieces you’ve locked.

  • Heaters 90 cm from fabric, 60 cm from where you sit
  • Daylight and 300 lux on the desk, door swing kept clear
  • Fan aimed at people, AC not at the bed, sofa facing the TV
