Vision

A weekend problem, solved before the weekend.

People rearrange a room a few times a year: moving into a hall, setting up a desk, buying a fan before a Hong Kong summer. RoomShift is the room-physics tool for those moments, priced for them, and good enough that designers use it all week.

Where we sit

Real physics, minutes of setup.

Room planners (magicplan, Polycam) make capture easy but show no physics. Engineering simulators (SimScale, DesignBuilder) have the physics but need an engineer. Consumer airflow apps sit between. RoomShift scans like a planner and simulates like a lab, in one browser tab.

Risks

What could go wrong, and how we’ll find out early.

“Is it accurate?”

Every lens names its model and its limits on the Science page, and every number is labelled an estimate. Calibration against measured fans comes before any accuracy claim.

People only need it once

That’s the premise, not a bug. The Room Pass is priced for one weekend; repeat use comes from moving, buying and designers.

Setup takes too long

Photo, AI, LiDAR, floor-plan and shop-link capture are all in the product. The test we run: can someone rebuild their furnished bedroom in under five minutes?

Willingness to pay is unknown

First test: a priced checkout shown to renters who’ve just finished a free session.
