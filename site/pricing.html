Questions

Before you buy.

Do I need an account?

No. Rooms save in your browser. Download a room file to move it to another device.

Does the AI scan cost extra?

The AI scan uses your own Anthropic API key, billed by Anthropic at their rates (a room photo is a few cents). The on-device photo, LiDAR and floor-plan routes are free and private.

How accurate are the numbers?

They’re planning estimates from published models: good for comparing layouts, not certified measurements. The Science page lists each model and its limits.

What happens after 7 days?

Nothing renews. Your room and its report stay yours; full-resolution features switch back to preview.

Will it run on my laptop?

Any recent Chrome, Edge or Safari. With WebGPU the airflow runs at 5 cm; without it, a lighter CPU preview kicks in automatically.
