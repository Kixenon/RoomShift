We checked it against a free jet. It doesn’t match.

  • Why: the model pushes air over a 1.8 m zone, and 2 s isn’t steady state.
  • So: trust the ranking between layouts, not the absolute speeds.
  • Next: steady state on the GPU, then calibrate to an anemometer at 0.5, 1, 2 and 3 m.
  • What holds: near-zero divergence, no flow through walls, a still room stays at 20 °C. All automated tests.

Every result is labelled.

Simulated

  • 3D incompressible airflow around solid furniture
  • Heat carried by the air, with buoyancy
  • Fans, heaters and open windows

Assumed

  • Fan power and noise rise with speed
  • Seated head-and-chest zones
  • Heater output in °C per second, not watts

Left out

  • Turbulence models and blade swirl
  • Steady state (2 s runs)
  • Wall heat storage, radiation, sun, HVAC
  • Humidity, body heat, perceived comfort
