Especially in a few hundred square feet, through a long Hong Kong summer.

Rearranging a room is cheap to get wrong on screen, and expensive to get wrong on the floor.

  • The fan in the corner cools mostly the wall behind it.
  • The heater behind the sofa warms air nobody sits in.
  • The lamp across the room puts your shadow on the page.

A furnished room, simulated in your browser.

  1. Size the room in metres
  2. Place what’s in it: sofa, bed, desk, lamp, fan, heater
  3. Add windows and open them
  4. Switch on Air, Heat or Light

Live. Not a recording.

Press L to load the editor here, or E to open it full screen.

No server. The solver runs on your GPU.

  • A full 3D editor in your browser. Size the room, place furniture, add windows, undo everything.
  • The solver runs on your own GPU. A Web Worker voxelises the room and solves it with WebGPU — nothing leaves your machine.
  • Air, heat and light update as you edit. Rendered as volumes, streamlines and real-time shadows.
